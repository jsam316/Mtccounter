📷 Setting up Count Assist

  • 📱Prop the phone side-on to the walking path so people cross the screen left↔right — not walking toward the camera.
  • 🚶Aim at a single-file stretch — the path where communicants return from the altar works best. Avoid the packed queue.
  • ┊Tap empty video to place the dashed counting line where only communicants cross — away from the choir and bystanders. People standing still are never counted.
  • 🔁Choose the direction to count. People moving the other way (returning) are ignored.
  • 👶Small children are skipped automatically — switch the toggle off if your parish counts them.
  • 👆Tap a person to count them as 👨 or 👩. The camera count is only a cross-check — your taps are the record.
  • 📶First use needs internet to download the detector (~6 MB) — open it once at home on Wi-Fi before the service. It works offline after that.
